# Final Pitch

## 30-Second Pitch
> Prediction market analytics tells you what happened. But when you're about to act, there's no verification that your action matches your intent. MarketLens solves this: analytics → Moss simulation → structured receipt → transparent intent checks. All unsigned. All verifiable. Simulation before signing, receipt before trust.

## 90-Second Pitch
> Onchain analytics dashboards show charts — unique wallets, D1 repeat, market volume. But the gap between "I see the data" and "I'm about to buy YES on Market 1" is where money gets lost.
>
> MarketLens closes that gap. You see product analytics. You form an intent — which market, which outcome, how much. Moss constructs the exact transaction. We simulate it via debug_traceCall on a local Anvil. The Receipt shows every event and transfer in order. Seven transparent intent checks verify: right buyer? right market? right outcome? payment within limits?
>
> All of this happens unsigned, not broadcast, state unchanged. The demo runs in 3 minutes at `/demo`. The simulation is real. The verification is real. What's not real yet: Monad deployment, wallet signing, broadcasting.
